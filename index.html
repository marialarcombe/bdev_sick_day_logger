BDEV Sick Day Logger

Internal payroll tool — absence tracking & report generation

  • 1 First time set-up: Click ⚙ Settings, enter your name (used in the email sign-off) and update the employee list for your team. Click the ✕ on any name to remove it, or + Add Employee to add new starters.
  • 2 Log an absence: Select the employee, then set the date range — starting from their last day worked before falling sick, through to their first day back. The app will display every day in that range.
  • 3 Mark each day: Use the four buttons on each row — Worked, Sickness, Holiday (approved planned leave), or Day off (never due to work). Only days marked as Sickness count as missed shifts.
  • 4 Generate & save: Click Generate Payroll Report to preview the formatted report. Check it looks right, then click Save & Start New Entry to store it. Repeat for each employee who was absent that month.
  • 5 Month end — email Laura: In the Saved Entries section, select the month from the dropdown, then click Open Email Draft. This opens your email app pre-addressed to Laura Brown with all that month's entries compiled in the correct format. Review and send.
  • ! Backup your data: Your entries are saved in your browser. If you clear your browser's cookies or site data, entries will be lost. Use the Export Backup button in Settings to download a copy regularly — especially before clearing your browser or switching computers. Use Import Backup to restore.
